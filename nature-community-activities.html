Beyond academics / Nature & conservation

Learning to care for nature.

Activities with Gia đình em yêu thiên nhiên Việt Nam · 2022–2023

Through activities with the nature-focused community group Gia đình em yêu thiên nhiên Việt Nam, I learned about sea turtle conservation and personally took part in releasing sea turtle hatchlings into the ocean in 2022. I also participated in hands-on tree planting in 2023. These experiences strengthened my appreciation for wildlife, ecosystems, and environmental stewardship.
