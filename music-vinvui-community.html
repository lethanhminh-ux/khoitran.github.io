Music in the community

Playing together, outdoors

In 2025, on selected weekends, I joined informal, open-air music sessions organized by the VinVui community at Vinhomes Central Park. Playing electric guitar alongside fellow musicians, I enjoyed sharing music with residents who gathered to listen, perform, and connect.

These spontaneous sessions reminded me that music is not only about individual practice or polished performances, but also about bringing people together.
