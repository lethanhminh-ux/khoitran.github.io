Christmas Celebration 2023

A ticketed community Christmas celebration at Vinhomes Central Park, Ho Chi Minh City.

Live ensemble · December 2023

Keyboard performance

Khoi performed on keyboard as part of a live band at a ticketed Christmas celebration held at Vinhomes Central Park in Ho Chi Minh City. The event had 1,000 tickets and brought together the community for an evening of festive performances.

The ticket figure refers to the event's tickets, not a verified audience attendance count.
