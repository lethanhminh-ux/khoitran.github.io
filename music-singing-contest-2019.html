Asian Primary School Singing Contest

Champion · “Nevada” by Vicetone

A performance shaped by independent initiative

In 2019, Khoi independently registered for the Asian Primary School Singing Contest, created his own choreography, and rehearsed on his own. He won first place with his performance of “Nevada” by Vicetone.
